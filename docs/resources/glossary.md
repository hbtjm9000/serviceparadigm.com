# Customer Glossary — How We Talk About AI Agents

We build AI tools for your business. This glossary defines what we mean when we use those terms — no hype, no marketing fluff, just what they are.

---

## A

**Agent** — See *AI Agent*.

**Agent Harness**
The runtime system that wraps an AI model with safety rails, tool access, and human checkpoints. The harness is where we enforce limits — not the model. Every Automata includes a harness.

**AI Agent**
An automated system that follows instructions, uses tools, and completes tasks within defined boundaries. It is not a person, not a chatbot, not magic. It is a machine built for work — composed of a model, a harness, tools, and a working environment. It handles *tasks*, not *jobs*.

**Assembly**
Multi-Automata orchestration. When you need several Automata working together across end-to-end processes. See also *Directorate*, *Bureau*.

**Automata (Ax)**
Our brand for autonomous AI agents. The name comes from "automaton" — a machine that follows a set of instructions. Not a person, not an employee. Automata handle invoicing, scheduling, procurement, and customer resolution. Self-hosted on Jamaican infrastructure.

---

## B

**Bureau**
The back-office category of Automata. A Bureau contains specific Desks — the actual workflows. See *Collections Desk*, *Schedule Desk*, *Procurement Desk*, *Resolution Desk*.

**Bootstrapped Agent**
A pre-configured AI Agent for a common business function. Delivered in 2 weeks at a fixed price. Includes working environment, harness, and one trained task loop.

---

## C

**Collections Desk**
An Automaton that handles invoicing, payment chasing, reminders, and reconciliation. Your collections process, automated end-to-end.

---

## D

**Desk**
A specific workflow within a Bureau. Each Desk is one task-focused Automaton. Example: Collections Desk, Schedule Desk.

**Directorate**
The coordination Automaton. It doesn't do the work itself — it monitors your Bureau Desks, escalates exceptions, and reports to your team. Think of it as the supervisor that never sleeps.

---

## F

**Factory**
Our build and deployment environment. Automata are constructed here, tested here, and shipped from here to your workspace.

---

## G

**Guided Execution**
A workflow where the agent proposes a plan, a human approves it, the agent executes, and a human verifies the result. No autonomy ambiguity — you stay in control.

---

## H

**Harness** — See *Agent Harness*.

**Harness Engineering**
The discipline of designing environments, constraints, and feedback loops that make AI agents reliable in production. Distinct from prompt engineering. This is what we do.

**Hybrid Inference**
Running AI models across both local (self-hosted) and cloud infrastructure, selected per-task based on sensitivity and speed requirements.

---

## K

**Knowledge Agent**
An AI Agent specialized in finding and explaining information from a defined knowledge base. Read-only by default — it answers questions but does not take action.

---

## L

**Laboratory**
Our research, training, and validation environment. Automata are tested here before they reach your Bureau.

---

## M

**Monitor Agent**
An AI Agent that watches systems, data, or processes and alerts on anomalies. Watch only — it does not make changes.

---

## O

**Orchestration Agent**
An agent that coordinates other agents. Delegates tasks, collects results, routes problems. Does not execute tasks directly.

---

## P

**Process Agent**
An AI Agent that spans an end-to-end business process (e.g. procurement, order-to-cash). Maintains state across steps and hands off between sub-agents.

**Procurement Desk**
An Automaton that handles vendor selection, purchase order creation, and delivery tracking. End-to-end procurement automation.

---

## R

**Resolution Desk**
An Automaton that handles customer inquiries, support tickets, and escalation. Keeps your customers informed while you handle the complex cases.

---

## S

**Schedule Desk**
An Automaton that manages appointments, calendar booking, and reminders. No more back-and-forth emails to find a time.

**Sovereign Deploy**
AI infrastructure deployed on customer-owned hardware or dedicated tenancy in Jamaican data centers. Your data never leaves the country. This is our default deployment model, not a premium add-on.

**Stack Ownership**
Owning your full AI stack — inference, memory, tooling — rather than renting from a single vendor. Prevents lock-in and vendor dependency.

---

## T

**Task Agent**
A focused AI Agent that executes one function well. The basic unit of deployment. A Task Agent handles a defined sequence of steps with tool access and human milestone check-ins.

**Tooling Layer**
The set of capabilities an agent can call: skills (procedures), plugins (third-party integrations), and MCP servers (external tools). This defines what the agent is allowed to do.

---

## V

**Verification Gate**
A mandatory human review step before agent output becomes action. Built into every Automata. Not a limitation — a design principle. You see what the agent produced before it takes effect.

---

## How We Use These Terms

- **On this site** — We use the Automata brand (Directorate, Bureau, Desk) because it's clear and honest about what you're buying.
- **In our blogs** — We use the engineering taxonomy (Task Agent, Process Agent, Orchestration Agent) when discussing architecture.
- **In your contract** — We use the delivery form (Single Desk, Multi-Desk, Assembly) because it maps to what you pay for.

They describe the same thing at different levels of detail. This glossary connects them.
