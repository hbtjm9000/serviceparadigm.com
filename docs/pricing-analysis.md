# Automata Pricing Analysis: Original Offers vs Desk-Based Approach

**Prepared:** July 2026
**Frameworks:** Profit First (Michalowicz) + MSP Standard Ratios (Kaseya/TBG) + Unit Economics
**Assumptions:** 29% gross taxes, 3 weeks paid vacation, US$60K target take-home salary

## 1. The Two Offerings Compared

### Original (Big Rock) — Services-Led Model

| Offer | Price | Type | What You Get |
|-------|-------|------|-------------|
| Ha Element | Unpriced | Capability | Base Hermes Agent unbundled from service wrappers |
| Jump Start | $999 USD | One-time onboarding | VPS provision, Hermes install, Honcho population, channel wiring, tooling, handoff |
| Managed Onboarding | $4,999 USD | One-time onboarding | Jump Start + NOC support, SLA monitoring, monthly steering sessions, patching, maintenance |

**Billing model:** One-time fees. Zero recurring revenue. No MRR. After the engagement, no ongoing income from that client unless you invent a retainer.

### Current Desk-Based (Automata) — Product-Led Model

| Offer | Onboarding (JMD) | Monthly (JMD) | USD Equiv/mo | Type | Scope |
|-------|-----------------|--------------|-------------|------|-------|
| Single Desk | $49,000 ($316) | $45,000 ($290) | $290/mo | Recurring | 1 workflow, 500 tx/mo |
| Multi-Desk | $89,000 ($574) | $85,000 ($548) | $548/mo | Recurring | 3 workflows, 2,000 tx/mo |
| Full Assembly | Custom | Custom | Custom | Recurring | Unlimited, orchestrated |

**Billing model:** Onboarding fee + monthly recurring. MRR-positive from day one with each client.

### Head-to-Head

| Dimension | Original (Services) | Desk-Based (Product) | Verdict |
|-----------|-------------------|---------------------|---------|
| **Revenue model** | One-time only | Recurring + onboarding | **Desk wins** — MRR compounds |
| **Y1 from 1 client** | $999 | $3,800 (single) / $7,155 (multi) | **Desk 3.8-7.2x higher** |
| **Y2 from same client** | $0 | $3,484 (single) / $6,581 (multi) | **Desk wins** — zero vs recurring |
| **Entry price** | $999 one-time | $316 onboarding + $290/mo | **Desk lower barrier** |
| **Scope clarity** | "Full setup" (vague) | "1 workflow, 500 tx" (specific) | **Desk wins** — clearer |
| **Tech stack** | Hermes Agent only | Automata platform | Different products |

### Critical Difference

The original Jump Start was effectively a **services engagement** — you paid $999 for a setup project, then owned the infrastructure with no ongoing obligation to Paradigm.

The Desk model is a **managed product** — you pay monthly for ongoing operation, support, and the platform itself. This aligns recurring revenue with recurring value delivery. The trade-off: you now carry delivery cost per customer per month.

The business must generate at least $90,423/year pre-tax just to pay the founder before any other costs — infrastructure, software, marketing, accounting, or growth.

### 2.2 Profit First Allocation Targets

For a business in the $0-$250K revenue band (where a new Automata practice starts), Profit First targets:

| Account | % of Revenue | Purpose |
|---------|-------------|---------|
| **Profit** | 5% | Reserve, quarterly bonuses |
| **Owner's Pay** | 50% | Your salary (covers the $84,507 gross) |
| **Tax** | 15% | Quarterly tax reserves |
| **Operating Expenses** | 30% | Infrastructure, tools, services, misc |

**Real Revenue = Total Revenue.** For a software/SaaS business with trivial COGS (no materials, no subcontractors), real revenue ≈ total revenue.

### 2.3 MSP Standard Ratios (Kaseya/Taylor Business Group)

| Ratio | Standard | What it means |
|-------|----------|---------------|
| Service salaries | ≤ 33% of revenue | Team cost ceiling |
| Gross margin on agreements | ≥ 65% | At least 65¢ of every dollar after direct costs |
| Admin expense | ≤ 20% of revenue | Office, software, non-billable staff |
| Sales expense | ≤ 10% of revenue | Marketing, sales headcount, commissions |
| Net operating income | ≥ 10% of revenue | True profit after everything |
| Service utilization | ≥ 75% billable | Techs should be billable 75%+ of time |

## 4. Pricing Reality Check

### 4.1 Current Desk Pricing in USD (at JMD 155/USD)

| Offer | Onboarding (USD) | Monthly (USD) | Annual (Y1) |
|-------|-----------------|--------------|------------|
| Single Desk | $316 | **$290** | $3,800 |
| Multi-Desk | $574 | **$548** | $7,155 |

### 4.2 How This Maps to MSP Standards

At $290/mo Single Desk:

| Check | Calculation | Pass? |
|-------|------------|-------|
| Gross margin ≥ 65% | Delivery cost must be ≤ $102/mo | Depends on delivery model |
| Labor ≤ 33% of revenue | Labor cost must be ≤ $96/mo | **Impossible with human-in-loop** |
| Net operating income ≥ 10% | Must keep $29/mo after all costs | Tight |

**The critical insight:** $290/mo is well below typical MSP per-user/per-device pricing in mature markets (US MSPs average $150-250/user/month for full managed services). For a task-specific AI agent with limited scope, this is a reasonable SME price point **if delivery is largely automated.**

### 4.3 Unit Economics: Single Desk

| Item | Amount |
|------|--------|
| Monthly revenue | $290 |
| Max delivery cost (65% GM) | **$102** |
| Hosting + inference cost est. | ~$10-20/mo |
| Remaining for support + margin | $82-92/mo |
| Hours of human support affordable | ~15 min/mo at $50/hr burdened |

**Key takeaway:** Single Desk only works if human touch is minimal — automated onboarding, self-service support, and exception-only escalation. If every Desk customer needs 2+ hours of human support per month, the margin collapses.

### 4.4 Exchange Rate Risk (Critical for JMD Pricing)

The current pricing is in JMD but salary costs are effectively USD-anchored (since US$60K must convert to JMD at market rates).

| JMD/USD Rate | Single Desk MRR (USD) | Multi-Desk MRR (USD) | Impact |
|-------------|----------------------|---------------------|--------|
| 140 (strong JMD) | $321 | $607 | Favorable |
| 155 (current) | $290 | $548 | Baseline |
| 170 (weak JMD) | $265 | $500 | **-9% revenue** |
| 185 (severe) | $243 | $459 | **-16% revenue** |

If the JMD weakens by 20% (to 185), your effective USD revenue drops 16% but your fixed USD salary cost stays the same. This squeezes margin on every customer. **JMD pricing without a USD indexation clause is a structural risk.**

## 5. TruMethods / MSP Framework Guidance

### 5.1 Key TruMethods Principles

1. **MRR is the only metric that matters.** One-time services don't compound. The Desk model correctly prioritizes recurring revenue.
2. **Professional services per engineer: ≥ $20,000/month.** Each technical person should generate at least $20K/mo in services revenue. At $290/customer, one engineer needs 69 customers to hit this.
3. **Price on value, not cost.** A Desk that saves a business 20 hours/month of admin time is worth far more than $290/mo. The pricing should reflect the value, not just what's cheap enough to sell.
4. **Tiered offerings simplify decisions.** The Desk model (Single → Multi → Assembly) follows this correctly.

### 5.2 What TruMethods Would Flag

- **Per-customer MRR too low for a scalable MSP.** $290/mo is acceptable at 100+ customers but painful at 20. Consider a minimum engagement of 2 Desks to raise average MRR.
- **No per-user or per-device component.** Pure flat-rate pricing leaves money on the table when clients grow. Consider transaction overage fees above the 500/2,000 thresholds.
- **Onboarding fee too low.** $316 onboarding for a setup that includes discovery, configuration, and training is probably below actual cost. The original Jump Start at $999 is closer to reality for the work involved.

## 6. Recommendations

### 6.1 Immediate Pricing Adjustments

| Action | Rationale | Impact |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| **Single Desk: JMD 59K/mo ($380)** | Current $290/mo requires 36 customers for viability. At $380, need 28 | -8 customers to breakeven |
| **Multi-Desk: JMD 115K/mo ($740)** | Better reflects value of 3 workflows vs 1 | Closer to MSP norms |
| **Onboarding: JMD 99K single / 149K multi ($640/$960)** | Aligns closer to real setup cost | Prevents negative-margin onboarding |
| **Add transaction overage** | 500 tx limit → $5/tx overage | Captures upside as clients grow |
| **Index pricing to USD or add FX adjustment clause** | JMD volatility erodes margin | Protects against currency risk |

### 6.2 Recommended Tier Structure

| Tier | Onboarding | Monthly | Best For |
|------|-----------|---------|---------|
| **Desk** (1 workflow, 500 tx) | JMD 99K ($640) | JMD 59K ($380) | Single pain point |
| **Bureau** (3 desks, 2K tx) | JMD 149K ($960) | JMD 115K ($740) | Growing business |
| **Directorate** (unlimited + orchestration) | JMD 299K ($1,930) | JMD 199K ($1,280) | Full back-office automation |

### 6.3 Volume Roadmap to Sustainability

| Phase | Timeframe | Customers | Monthly Revenue | Founder Take-Home |
|-------|-----------|-----------|----------------|-------------------|
| Launch | Month 0-3 | 3-5 | $1,740-2,900 | Draw from savings |
| Ramp | Month 4-9 | 10-15 | $5,800-8,700 | Partial salary |
| Stable | Month 10-18 | 20-28 | $11,600-16,240 | Full salary (PF) |
| Sustainable | Year 2 | 30-40 | $17,400-23,200 | Salary + profit + reserve |
| Growth | Year 3 | 60-80+ | $34,800-46,400 | Team, marketing, scale |

At the recommended pricing (Bureau anchor at $740/mo), targets drop:
- Survival: 19 customers (vs 36 at current pricing)
- Minimal team: 28 customers (vs 53)
- Sustainable: 45 customers (vs 87)

### 6.4 What I'd Do First

1. **Raise Single Desk to JMD 59K/mo** — the value justifies it. A Desk that replaces a part-time admin saves more than $380/mo.
2. **Replace "Single/Multi/Full Assembly" with "Desk/Bureau/Directorate"** — consistent with the brand taxonomy on the resources page.
3. **Add a minimum 2-Desk commitment for new clients** — raises ACV, lowers churn risk, simplifies support.
4. **Quote in JMD with a USD floor** — protects against FX erosion without requiring USD pricing that feels foreign to Jamaican SMEs.
5. **Target 5 customers/month during ramp** — 6 months to breakeven instead of 12.
